                      We present the results of exact wave-function calculations that are equivalent to numerical experi-
                   ments on a two-electron quantum system. We show that Rabi oscillations of the core electron can modi-
                   fy the photoelectron spectrum of the outer electron by efficient coherence transfer via e-e correlation,
                   even in the absence of an autoionizing level or other continuum structure. We compare a simplified
                   analytically soluble two-electron theory with our numerical results.
